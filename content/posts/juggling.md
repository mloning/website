I first learned to juggle three balls at university, together with a group of friends during our exam period.
During the following summer break, I kept practicing.
I would walk my dog, holding his leash in one hand and juggling two balls in the other.
When I returned for the next term, I could juggle four balls.

Five balls, however, seems to require serious practice.
While I felt like I’ve been close at times, I have never quite managed to master it.

I use two types of juggling balls:

- [Play](https://playjuggling.com), not exactly sure which model, but soft shell, filled with millet seed, ~62 mm (diameter), 100 g, bought at [Rouge et Noir](https://rouge-et-noir.fr/) in Paris
- Soft shell, ~68mm (diameter), bought at [441 Malabares](https://441malabares.com/) in Madrid

Here are some useful resources:

- [Library of Juggling](https://libraryofjuggling.com/), explanations and animations for many juggling patterns ([HN thread 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46853552), [HN thread 2024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39601201))
- [Most people can't juggle one ball](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/jTGbKKGqs5EdyYoRc/most-people-can-t-juggle-one-ball) ([HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47702887))

There is also [bounce juggling](https://juggle.fandom.com/wiki/Bounce_juggling), but I only do toss juggling.
