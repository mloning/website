Some of my favourite films:

- [Chronique des Années de Braise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicle_of_the_Years_of_Fire) (Algerian war of independence)
- Lawrence of Arabia (biographical drama about the Arab uprising during World War 1; androgyny/heteronormativity, cultural immersion/identity, orientalism, colonialism, Messiah figure)
- Metal y Melancolía (documentary interviewing Peruvian taxi drivers during the crisis in the 90s)
- Inland Empire by David Lynch
- Boyhood (coming-of-age drame filmed over 10 years)
- The Silence of Others (documentary about the silenced victims of the Franco regime)
- [The Spirit of the Beehive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Spirit_of_the_Beehive) (Spanish civil war)
- [Aftersun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftersun)
- [The Stories We Tell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stories_We_Tell) (documentary about family relationships and secrets)
- [Visages, Villages](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_Places_(film)>) by Agnès Varda
- Volver by Almodóvar

Some of my favourite series:

- Chernobyl (historical drama about the Chernobyl disaster)
- Underground Railroad (slavery in the US)
